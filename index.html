WELCOME TO THE HIGH ROAD

Two sides.
A little more gravity.

New: Everyone drives. Choose 2–4 players. P1 uses WASD, P2 arrows, P3 IJKL, P4 TFGH. Each gets full steering, gas and brake/reverse; the final active seat is the known saboteur. Inputs are weighted and summed, not averaged. Above normal input, a soft limit caps agreement at about 1.35×. Normal steering is stable and speed-sensitive; collisions and falls remain physical. Extra sabotage powers are disabled in this mode.

Weights start at 0.5 each for four players, 0.8 / 0.8 / 0.6 for three, and 1.4 / 0.7 for two. Adjust them before a round. Solo rehearsal bypasses weights and inactive seats: WASD controls one arcade car. Or choose Bot or a connected standard controller for each seat. Gamepads use left stick/D-pad steering, RT/R2 gas, and LT/L2 brake/reverse. Click the driving view after editing settings.

Try the forest fork skips ahead: straight follows the longer, wide loop; left takes a shorter, narrow cut-through. Both rejoin before the village checkpoint. This first fork is a fixed tradeoff, not yet a changing traffic/puzzle choice.

In shared mode, one driver uses W / S for the left wheels. The other uses ↑ / ↓ for the right wheels.

Hold both accelerators to go straight. More left power turns right; more right power turns left. Brake while moving forward; keep holding to reverse. Power builds and fades gradually.

Flying solo? Switch to Solo test: W/S drive both sides, and A/D adjust the balance to turn.

Player three: J surges the left wheels, L surges the right, and K makes all tires slippery. Every attack has a warning. Drivers can brake against a surge or slow down before grip drops. Energy regenerates; attacks cannot overlap.

Start a four-minute round for results. Practice shortcuts and tuning are locked during rounds. Pausing freezes traffic, the clock and all abilities. Recovery cancels attacks and grants four seconds of protection. Swap the saboteur between rounds.

Follow nine arches: climb the hill, cross the wooden bridge, wind through the forest, share the village road with two-way traffic, then climb Orchard Hill and coast toward the finish. Keep right in the village. Locals join and leave via depot roads, slow for cars ahead, and can collide with you.

Try a section: Clifftop, Bridge start and Village traffic skip you ahead. Water falls and settled upside-down landings return you to the last safe checkpoint. Land upright on dry ground and keep driving. R restarts the whole course.
